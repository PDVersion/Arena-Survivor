Arena Survivor device check

This is an instrumented test build. It does not establish production performance or older-device acceptance by itself.

Load a scenario, choose Default or Low in the game’s Settings, then record. Select upgrades and resume pauses normally.

Keep the game visible and focused. The recorder waits for active play, warms up for 10 seconds, and measures at least 60 seconds. It stops within three minutes. Interruptions, pauses and frame stalls stay in the downloaded evidence. Run each 60/150/300 scenario three times in both Default and Low; also play normally, resize, pause/resume and restart. A complete human playable run remains a separate checklist item.
