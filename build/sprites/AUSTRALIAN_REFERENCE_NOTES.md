# Familiar Australian sprite construction references

Owner direction 2026-10-23: recognisable everyday items take priority over unusual
angular/fantasy silhouettes. These are appearance references and contextual
examples, not statistically measured Sydney prevalence or disposal guidance.
The existing game IDs, behaviours, tuning and material families do not change.

## Primary context reviewed 2026-10-23

- [City of Ryde household A–Z](https://www.ryde.nsw.gov.au/Environment-and-Waste/Waste-and-Recycling/Waste-Resources/A-to-Z-Guide-of-Waste-and-Recycling)
  explicitly lists wine/soft-drink bottles and pasta-sauce jars as ordinary glass
  containers. Search-index excerpt reviewed; direct fetch403. Do not claim a full
  visual inspection of that page.
- [NSW Return and Earn container examples](https://returnandearn.org.au/what-you-can-return)
  names water/soft-drink/juice/sports-drink plastic bottles, glass beer/water/
  soft-drink/juice/kombucha bottles and drink cans as on-the-go containers.
  This use is visual context only, not an eligibility lesson in the game.
- [Visy 750ml Bordeaux bottle specification](https://productcatalogue.visy.com/products/750ml-bvs-bordeaux/pdf)
  confirms a 750ml wine-container form (295 mm tall,74.5 mm diameter) and green/flint
  glass. Use a generic cylinder/shoulder/long-neck silhouette, no copied label,
  logo, proprietary surface graphics or claims about market share.
- [Clean Up Australia litter report](https://www.cleanup.org.au/litter-report/)
  gives contextual recognition support for cigarette butts and ordinary litter.
  Existing catalog candidates remain selected; percentages never become spawn stats.
- [City of Sydney bulky collection examples](https://www.cityofsydney.nsw.gov.au/waste-recycling-services/book-pick-up-for-bulky-items)
  includes furniture, mattresses, whitegoods and household bulk. A dumped mound
  should contain recognisable worn furniture/cartons/bags, not a fantasy golem.
- [Australian litter-picker construction](https://www.bunnings.com.au/jantex-litter-picker-grooved-jaw-rotating-head-82cm-grabber-rubbish-indoor-outdoor_p1222618)
  supports a trigger grip, straight shaft and grooved gripping jaws. Search-index
  product excerpt reviewed; direct fetch unavailable.
- [Wet/dry vacuum hose construction](https://www.bunnings.com.au/cleanstar-complete-vacuum-cleaner-hose-for-various-models-36mm-wet-dry_p1054850)
  describes a corrugated plastic hose/straight nozzle. Use ordinary vacuum shapes,
  not a science-fiction weapon or a claim that this tool handles all real waste.

## Authored visual choices (inferences, not source measurements)

Glass Bottle: green/teal ordinary wine bottle with rounded cylindrical body,
recognisable shoulders, long narrow neck and plain cap; a pasta-sauce/jam jar
is a later equally familiar option, not a new role automatically. Preserve a3
as comparison and generate a4 as the familiar-shape alternative.

Bag: charcoal household sack, tied knot and broad sagging plastic folds; violet
is an accent, not an unexplained bright magical body. Can: familiar cylindrical
aluminium beverage container with rolled rims and tab end. Wrapper: crinkled
chips/confectionery packet with sealed ends. Butt: one small tan/orange filter,
white paper and dark spent tip. Shards: curved broken container glass, not crystals.

Player: practical council/volunteer cleanup workwear, visible human face/hood,
gloves and boots; no radiation suit, military armour or robot. Tools: actual
litter-grabber shaft/trigger/claws and vacuum canister/hose/nozzle, with cyan/green
accents. Evolution stays recognisably the same tool. Sites: clothing rail,
cardboard takeaway packaging, wooden pallet/cartons, ordinary refuse enclosure.
Spill/debris/vent: believable ground/infrastructure details with restrained game
signals. The pickup remains an explicitly fictional gameplay token.

These prompts simplify real objects into pixel art; no branding, text, sourced
fact panel, recycling eligibility, chemical handling advice or extra mechanics.
First-pass generation is the current goal; individual art/live revisions follow
owner testing. Keep sources and exact augmented prompts for every attempt.
